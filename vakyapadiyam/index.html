Book 1

ब्रह्मकाण्ड

Brahmakāṇḍa

Establishes the metaphysical foundation of Śabda-Brahman. The world is an apparent transformation (vivarta) of the eternal Word.

45+ Kārikās
Book 2

वाक्यकाण्ड

Vākyakāṇḍa

Focuses on the sentence (vākya) as the primary, indivisible unit of meaning. Explores the Sphoṭa theory of language.

~490 Kārikās
Book 3

पदकाण्ड

Padakāṇḍa

Detailed analysis of words (pada), their components, and how language categories (kārakas, time, action) shape our cognition.

~1325 Kārikās
Featured Verse

अनादिनिधनं ब्रह्म शब्दतत्त्वं यदक्षरम् ।
विवर्तते ऽर्थभावेन प्रक्रिया जगतो यतः ॥

anādinidhanaṃ brahma śabdatattvaṃ yadakṣaram |
vivartate 'rthabhāvena prakriyā jagato yataḥ ||

"That Brahman, which is without beginning or end, whose very essence is the Word, which is the indestructible imperishable, which manifests through objects — from That proceeds the creation of the world."

— Brahmakāṇḍa 1.1
