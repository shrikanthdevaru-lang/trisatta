### Publisher's & Translator's Preface
Although there exist numerous spiritual tools, vows, rituals, and practices by which seekers navigate the ocean of worldly existence (*bhava-sāgara*) through their own intellect, strength, knowledge, and devotion, the future outcome of actions often remains shrouded in doubt and uncertainty. Consequently, over time, countless paths, sects, and ritualistic doctrines arise. 

However, that alone is worthy of being accepted as Absolute Truth (*Satya*) which yields direct, observable, and empirical results (*Pratyakṣa-guṇa*).

This sacred treatise, **Śiva Svarodaya**, containing the profound dialogue between Goddess Pārvatī (Umā) and Supreme Lord Śiva (Maheśvara), reveals the direct science of the Elements (*Tattva-Jñāna*). By mastering this science—regulating the breath, inspecting the *nāḍīs*, and practicing *prāṇāyāma*—numerous yogis have perfected their lives and manifested the divine power of the Almighty within themselves.

In ancient times, authentic Sanskrit manuscripts of this priceless science were exceedingly rare and difficult to obtain. Recognizing its immense value for humanity, we painstakingly procured an authentic, pure Sanskrit exemplar and commissioned an accessible, clear commentary in Hindi (*Bhāṣāntara*). 

While physical, mental, and verbal actions are continuously performed by all beings, this science elevates a person from an ordinary human being (*nara-rāja*) to a sovereign god-like state (*deva-rāja*). Anyone desiring unshakeable peace, prosperity, and health in this world must study this science with faith in the Guru and dedication of mind. Such a practitioner will enjoy supreme bodily and spiritual happiness, and ultimately attain the eternal realm of bliss.
