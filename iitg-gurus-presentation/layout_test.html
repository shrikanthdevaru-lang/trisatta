RISHI GAUTAMA

NYAYA DARSANA

The Science of Right Knowledge
"Nyaya is the systematic inquiry into reality through valid means of knowledge, leading to true understanding and liberation."
ABOUT NYAYA DARSANA
Also known as Akṣapāda (meaning "one who has eyes on his feet," signifying his absolute, unbroken absorption in philosophical contemplation), Maharṣi Gautama is the illustrious founder of the Nyāya Darśana.
CORE CONTRIBUTIONS OF NYAYA DARSANA

Nyāya Sūtras

Authored the foundational text establishing the definitive framework for Indian logic.

Four Pramāṇas

Formalized perception, inference, comparison, and reliable testimony as valid knowledge.

INFLUENCE THAT CONTINUES

Intellectual Arena

Established the ground rules for all ancient Indian philosophical debates.
