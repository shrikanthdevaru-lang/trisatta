Bharata Muni

Era: Antiquity Vidyāsthāna: Nāṭya Śāstra, Alaṃkāra Śāstra (Aesthetics)

Life & Context

Revered as a semi-divine sage, he was requested by Brahmā to create a 'Pañcama Veda' (Fifth Veda) utilizing audio-visual arts to make high spiritual truths accessible to all people (Sādhāraṇīkaraṇa), regardless of their Varna or education.

Key Contributions

He composed the Nāṭyaśāstra, a massive encyclopedia governing stage design, music, dance mudras, and acting. Most profoundly, he formulated the 'Rasa Siddhānta' (Rasa Theory), arguing that the ultimate purpose of art is to evoke a transcendental state of aesthetic bliss in the spectator.

Legacy

Bharata Muni is the absolute patriarch of all Indian classical performing arts (music, dance, theatre). His psychological and physiological analysis of human emotion (Bhāva) and its aesthetic culmination (Rasa) remains a globally studied masterpiece of art philosophy.
